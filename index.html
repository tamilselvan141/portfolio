Case Study

Global Summit Coordination Platform

Simplifying complex meeting coordination.

B2B, SAAS, WEB

SITUATION

The event team was managing high-stakes meetings between international principals and liaison officers across multiple stakeholders, making coordination complex and difficult to track.

TASK

Design a centralized platform to simplify meeting coordination, manage assignments, and give teams better visibility into schedules and responsibilities.

ACTION

Designed the platform end-to-end across principal management, liaison workflows, meetings, scheduling, alerts, and reporting. Used AI-assisted wireframes to validate the direction early with the client before finalizing the design.

RESULT

Created a scalable coordination system that brought key workflows into one place, improving visibility across principals, liaison officers, meetings, and event operations.

Humini Biz

Making employee engagement simple, human, and meaningful.

0-1, B2B, SAAS, MOBILE APP

SITUATION

Employee engagement was largely driven by scheduled activities and formal initiatives. These often felt forced and didn't create enough opportunities for meaningful, everyday interactions between employees.

TASK

Explore how engagement could become more natural and accessible, while creating an experience that allows employees to participate without adding pressure or disrupting their work.

ACTION

Conducted interviews with 10+ employees across MNCs and mid-sized companies, synthesized key behavioral insights, defined HMWs and MVP priorities, and designed the employee and admin experiences from concept to high-fidelity prototype.

RESULT

Created a 0→1 employee engagement platform focused on low-effort interactions and meaningful moments. Presented the concept at a Singapore business expo, gaining prospective interest and actionable feedback.

Freshcots

A job platform designed to help opportunities find the right talent.

0-1, B2C, SAAS

SITUATION

Traditional job portals often favor conventional career paths, making it harder for freshers, career switchers, returners, and interns to stand out beyond their resumes.

TASK

Design a 0→1 job platform that creates a more inclusive hiring experience while making candidate discovery and evaluation easier for employers.

ACTION

Designed the product end-to-end, from research and user flows to wireframes, UI, and prototyping. Built tailored experiences for different candidate journeys and employer workflows, including profile discovery, expert support, and hiring interactions.

RESULT

Delivered a scalable, development-ready platform that went live, establishing a differentiated hiring experience where opportunities can discover relevant talent instead of relying only on traditional job search.
